# 📊 Contract & Provider Performance Dashboard

This dashboard provides a comprehensive view of contract performance, provider efficiency, and cost dynamics across the organization. It connects contract values, renewal behavior, service requests, and expert profiles to reveal how strategic decisions impact financial and operational outcomes.

The visuals highlight negotiation effectiveness, showing how counteroffers influence total contract value, while renewal and termination trends reflect provider stability and long-term partnership potential. Cost analysis by service type and requester uncovers key spending drivers, particularly in cloud and analytics services.

Provider-level insights compare accepted contracts, renewal rates, and total contract value, helping identify high-performing vendors. Additional analysis links expertise levels with offer prices, demonstrating how experience drives cost and value.

Overall, this dashboard transforms complex contract data into a clear narrative that supports data-driven decision-making, vendor optimization, and contract lifecycle management.
