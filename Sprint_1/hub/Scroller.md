## Overview

This report explains how the **Scroller visual** is imported and applied
in Power BI to display the **Sum of Sales and Sum of Profit by State**
in a dynamic, scrolling format. The Scroller visual is useful for
showcasing large numeric KPIs in a compact, continuously moving display.
