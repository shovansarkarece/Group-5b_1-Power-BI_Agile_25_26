# 📊 How to Create a Bar Chart in Power BI

Creating a bar chart in Power BI is simple and visual. Follow the steps below with icons for clarity.

---

## 🟦 **Step 1: Load Your Data**

1. 📁 Open **Power BI Desktop**
2. 🔽 Go to **Home → Get Data**
3. 📊 Choose a data source (Excel, CSV, SQL, Web, etc.)
4. ✔️ Select your file → Click **Load**
5. 📊 After that this type of UI we can see below in Powe Bi dashboard

## 📌 **Step 2: Insert a Bar Chart Visual**

1. ➡️ Go to the **Visualizations** pane on the right
2. Choose one of the bar chart icons:

   * 📊 **Clustered Bar Chart**
   * 📊 **Stacked Bar Chart**
   * 📊 **100% Stacked Bar Chart**
3. 🖥️ After that this type of UI we can see below in Powe Bi dashboard

## 🧩 **Step 3: Add Data Fields to the Chart**

Drag our fields into:

* 🏷️ **Axis** → Category (Examples: Product, Country, Month)
* 🔢 **Values** → Numerical data (Examples: Sales, Revenue, Quantity)
* 🎨 **Legend (optional)** → Comparison categories (Examples: Year, Region)

## 🎨 **Step 4: Customize Your Chart**

Click the **Format (🎨 paintbrush icon)** to style the chart.

### You can customize:

* 🎨 **Data Colors** → Change bar colors
* 🏷️ **Title** → Rename or format the title text
* 📏 **X-axis / Y-axis** → Labels, size, ranges
* 🔤 **Data Labels** → Turn on to display values on bars
* 📑 **Legend** → Position, colors, size
* 🧱 **Background & Border** → Add or remove borders
* ⚙️ **Effects** → Shadows, glow, visual styling

## 🔍 **Step 5: Add Enhancements (Optional)**

* 🔎 **Tooltips** – show extra info when hovering
* ⬇️ **Filters & Slicers** – interactively filter data
* ↕️ **Sort Bars** – sort ascending/descending
* ➕ **Drill-Down** – explore deeper levels (e.g., Year → Month → Day)

---

## ☁️ **Step 6: Save and Publish**

1. 💾 Click **File → Save As**
2. ☁️ Click **Publish** → Select your workspace
3. 🌍 Share your dashboard with your team
