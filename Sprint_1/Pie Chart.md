

# 🥧 How to Create a Pie Chart in Power BI
---

## 🟦 **Step 1: Load Your Data**

1. 📁 Open **Power BI Desktop**
2. 🔽 Go to **Home → Get Data**
3. 📊 Choose your data source (Excel, CSV, SQL, etc.)
4. ✔️ Click **Load** to import the data

## 🟣 **Step 2: Select the Pie Chart Visual**

1. ➡️ Go to the **Visualizations** pane
2. Click on the **Pie Chart** icon: 🥧
3. A blank pie chart visual will appear on your canvas

## 🧩 **Step 3: Add Data Fields**

You need **two fields**:

* **Legend (Category)** → groups (Examples: Product, Country, Department)
* **Values (Numbers)** → quantity or measure (Examples: Sales, Revenue, Count)

Bifurcation (or Partition) in a pie chart simply means:

👉 Dividing the whole pie into different parts or slices based on categories.

## 🎨 **Step 4: Customize the Pie Chart**

Click the **Format (🎨 paintbrush icon)** to style your chart.

### Customize options:

* 🎨 **Data Colors** → Change slice colors
* 🔤 **Data Labels** → Show values, percentage, or category
* 🏷️ **Title** → Rename and format chart title
* ➗ **Detail Labels** → Show category, value, percentage
* 📑 **Legend** → Turn on/off or reposition
* 💠 **Rotation** → Spin chart to adjust starting angle
* 🧱 **Background & Border** → Add styling
